About Our Project

Understanding the next generation of digital voting.

Smart Voting System

Smart Voting System is a final-year engineering project designed to demonstrate a secure and modern digital voting platform.

It integrates biometric verification, multi-factor authentication, location capture and blockchain technology to explore security and transparency in digital election workflows.

Our Technology

  • AI-Based Face Recognition
  • Liveness Detection
  • SMS OTP Authentication
  • GPS Location Tracking
  • Hyperledger Fabric Blockchain

Our Key Features

Advanced technologies planned for our voting platform.

Face Recognition

AI-based facial verification using FaceNet512 to support voter identity checks.

Liveness Detection

Motion and facial checks designed to help detect basic photo and replay attacks.

SMS OTP Verification

Additional account verification through one-time passwords sent by SMS.

GPS Tracking

Permission-based GPS location capture for registration and voting activity review.

Blockchain Security

Hyperledger Fabric integration for tamper-evident transaction records.

Admin Dashboard

Centralized administration, voter review, election management and activity monitoring.
